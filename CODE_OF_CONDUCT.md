# Code of Conduct

PowerScroll is built in the open, and everyone participating in its community
is expected to help make that community welcoming, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Give useful context, accept good-faith questions, and assume good intent.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at
constantin.chabirand@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
with appropriate confidentiality. The maintainer may edit or remove content,
issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants when needed
to protect the community.

This code applies in the repository, Discussions, issue tracker, and other
spaces where someone is representing PowerScroll.
